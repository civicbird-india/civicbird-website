Our safety principles

Know who you are dealing with.

Verification, careful handling of personal information and clear task details are important parts of a safer service experience.

Identity and verification checks

Bird applications should be reviewed and required identity and police-verification checks completed before activation. Do not treat an unverified profile as approved.

Personal information deserves care

Share sensitive documents only through an approved, secure process. Avoid sending Aadhaar images or passwords through public comments or unsecured channels.

Clear scope and charges

Review the task, service fee, additional costs and terms before work begins. Ask for clarification if anything is unclear.

Report a concern

Contact CivicBird if a profile, request or interaction raises a safety concern. Use official emergency services for immediate danger.

What is CivicBird?

CivicBird is a planned assistance service intended to help people navigate selected civic, documentation and local task requests. Available services and coverage should be confirmed before booking.

How much does a standard service cost?

The planned standard service fee is ₹300 plus 18% GST, which totals ₹354. Official department fees and other agreed charges may be additional and should be disclosed before you proceed.

Does CivicBird guarantee government approvals?

No. Decisions, eligibility, approvals and processing times are determined by the relevant government department or authority. CivicBird cannot guarantee an outcome.

Are all Birds police verified?

The intended policy is to activate Birds only after required identity and police-verification checks are completed. This page alone does not confirm that a particular applicant has been verified. Check a Bird's status through CivicBird before proceeding.

What do I need to submit a request?

Typically, you should describe the task, provide a reachable contact method and share relevant source and destination details. Only submit sensitive documents when they are required and a secure submission method is available.

Can I apply to become a Bird?

Yes, you can express interest using the contact form. The planned application fee is ₹149. The actual application process, applicable taxes, verification and terms must be confirmed before payment.

Can I pay online on this page?

No payment gateway is connected in this standalone homepage file. Do not enter card, UPI or banking details here. Online payments should be enabled only after a secure payment provider is integrated and tested.
