How it works

A straightforward way to request everyday assistance.

Tell us what you need

Choose a service category and describe your task honestly.

Enter your route

Share source and destination addresses. Location access is optional and only requested when you choose it.

Review the estimate

See service charge, estimated travel and 18% GST. Final charges are confirmed before the booking is accepted.

Team confirmation

The team checks service area, availability and assignment. A request is not confirmed until you receive confirmation.

Receive assistance

Check the assigned Bird's identity and contact support if anything seems different from your booking.

Feedback and support

Contact support for concerns, billing questions or feedback after the task.

Payments and online booking submission will be activated only after secure backend and Razorpay integration. Do not treat this demo as a live booking.

Kaam aapka, saath hamara. Making everyday citizen assistance easier, clearer and more accessible.
