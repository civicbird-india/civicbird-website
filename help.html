Quick answers

Frequently asked questions


Basic information about CivicBird service requests, fees and support.







What is CivicBird?

CivicBird is a citizen-assistance service intended to help people with selected government and private-sector office tasks. Service availability and the scope of a task must be confirmed before relying on the service.





What is the standard service fee?

The published standard fee is ₹300 plus 18% GST (₹54), totalling ₹354 for one task, subject to the applicable service terms. Any separately applicable charge should be explained before confirmation.





Does submitting this form create an official complaint?

No. At present, this page only prepares a summary in your browser. It does not transmit or store the form, create a ticket, or send an acknowledgement. Contact CivicBird directly using the listed contact details.





How do I check my booking status?

Use an official booking reference if one has been provided, and share it when contacting support. This page cannot look up bookings or show live booking status yet.





Can CivicBird guarantee that an office task will be completed?

No. Government and private offices control their own procedures, decisions, timelines and approvals. CivicBird assistance does not guarantee an approval or a particular outcome.





What should I do if I have a safety concern?

If there is immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service. For a non-emergency concern related to CivicBird, select “Safety or conduct” in the form and contact CivicBird directly. Do not wait for a website form to be reviewed.





Is my support request stored on this website?

No. This page does not send the form to a server or save it to a database. The summary is created locally in your current browser session and can be copied manually.
