Four simple stages

How CivicBird is designed to work

The goal is to make each step easy to understand, without making customers depend on email or WhatsApp to place a request.

STEP 01

Choose your service

Select the type of assistance you need. Explain the task clearly and add the source, destination or office details when relevant.

STEP 02

Review the details

Check the information you entered and review the stated service fee and GST before proceeding.

STEP 03

Receive a request status

When the online booking backend is connected, a submitted request can receive a unique reference number and status in your account.

STEP 04

Follow the progress

After the customer and staff portals are operational, customers will be able to check assignments and progress on the website.

STEP 05

View the final bill

Once the billing workflow is connected, the final bill and payment status will be available with the completed request.

STEP 06

Keep a clear record

The planned account system will keep request references, status history and bill records together for easier follow-up.

Designed for clarity

Know what happens at each stage.

CivicBird aims to make the process understandable for busy people, older residents and people who may find it difficult to visit an office themselves.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about the planned CivicBird process.

How much does a standard task cost?

The stated CivicBird service fee is ₹300 plus 18% GST (₹54), making ₹354 in total for the service fee. Any separate government fee or additional charge must be clearly disclosed where applicable.

Will I receive a token or request number?

Unique request numbers are part of the planned online booking system. They should only be issued after the backend successfully saves a request; the current review-only form does not create an official number.

Can I track my request online?

Online tracking is planned for the customer account area and will be available after the database and booking status features are connected and tested.

Do I need to send an email or WhatsApp message to book?

The intended customer booking workflow is website-based. The current form is for reviewing details only; the online submission backend is still to be connected.

Does CivicBird guarantee that every government request will be approved?

No. CivicBird can assist with eligible tasks, but decisions, approvals and processing times remain with the relevant authority. Never provide false information or misrepresent a representative's authority.

What if a task requires official fees or original documents?

Requirements vary by task and authority. Any official fee, document requirement or handling arrangement should be explained before proceeding. Do not share passwords, OTPs or banking credentials.
