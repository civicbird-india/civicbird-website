About CivicBird

A simple idea: make everyday citizen assistance easier to access.

OUR AIM

Make time-consuming tasks feel manageable.

CivicBird aims to connect people who need practical, everyday assistance with screened local helpers—our “Birds”—through a clearer process, transparent estimates and a support contact. The goal is to reduce avoidable running around and make help easier to request.

We are building CivicBird step by step. Service availability, verification and booking confirmation must be checked by our team; we do not promise government approval or special access to official departments.

Our principles

  • Clarity in pricing and communication
  • Respect for customers and workers
  • Careful identity and background checks
  • Privacy-conscious handling of information
  • Continuous improvement through feedback
M. S. UmamFounder & Chief Executive Officer

A vision built around people

M. S. Umam founded CivicBird with a simple ambition: make everyday assistance easier to find, easier to understand and more dependable for ordinary citizens. From errands and appointments to practical help that saves time, CivicBird aims to bring customers and responsible local helpers together through a clear, organised process.

The long-term vision is to build a trusted citizen-assistance network that values dignity, transparent pricing, safety checks and accountability. The goal is not to promise shortcuts or special treatment, but to help people navigate everyday tasks with less confusion and unnecessary running around.
