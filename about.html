CivicBird is being developed to help people coordinate everyday civic, documentation and office-related tasks with clearer expectations and respectful assistance.

Why CivicBird exists

Many everyday tasks involve unfamiliar forms, office visits, follow-ups and unclear next steps. CivicBird aims to help people understand the process and coordinate assistance, initially in Bhagalpur, Bihar.

Our principles are simple: clear communication, transparent pricing, careful handling of personal information and honest status updates.

What to expect

  • 1A clear description of the requested task.
  • 2A standard fee of ₹300 plus GST, with additional costs explained separately.
  • 3A request is pending until CivicBird explicitly confirms it.

Our service area

Initial focus: Bhagalpur, Bihar. Availability depends on task type, location, staffing and confirmation.
