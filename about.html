About CivicBird

A more organised way to get everyday assistance.

CivicBird is a citizen assistance business being built around a simple idea: people should be able to understand the next step in an everyday civic, documentation or local task without unnecessary confusion.

Our promise is not that every government process will be easy or every application will be approved. Our commitment is to communicate clearly, explain service expectations and treat customers and their information responsibly.

Our purpose

Why CivicBird exists

For many people, completing an office-related or documentation task can mean figuring out which office to visit, what information to prepare, where to go next and what charges may apply. These steps can be especially difficult for busy families, older residents and people who live away from the place where a task needs to be completed.

CivicBird aims to organise eligible assistance requests through a clear process: understand the task, explain what information may be needed, review the scope and communicate the next step. Our initial focus is Bhagalpur, Bihar, with the service area and availability to be confirmed for each request.

“KAAM AAP KA SAATH HAMARA”

For us, this means standing alongside customers with practical assistance and honest expectations. It does not mean claiming government authority or promising an approval that only the relevant department can decide.

Our principles

How we want to earn trust

We want our actions and customer experience to support the words on this page.

Say what is included

Explain the service scope, standard fee and any additional charges before the customer agrees to proceed.

Do not promise outcomes we cannot control

Government approvals, eligibility and processing times are decided by the relevant authority.

Respect customer information

Collect only information needed for the task and use an appropriate secure process for sensitive documents.

Keep improving

Build the website, request records, tracking and customer support in stages, testing each feature before describing it as live.

Our service approach

Clarity before commitment

The planned standard CivicBird service fee is ₹300 plus 18% GST (₹54), totalling ₹354 for the service fee. Government department fees and any additional agreed costs are separate unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Before a task is accepted, customers should be able to understand the requested work, what is included, the applicable charges and any limits on what CivicBird can do.
