Service questions

Before you submit a request.

What is included in the ₹354 service fee?

The standard service fee is ₹300 plus 18% GST, totalling ₹354. Official department fees and any separately agreed travel or task-related charges are not included unless clearly stated.

Why do I need to share my live location?

Your current location helps identify where the request is being made. Your browser will ask permission before sharing coordinates. You can deny permission, but the location field is required to submit this request form.

Is my booking confirmed as soon as I submit the form?

No. The request details need to be reviewed and availability, service scope and applicable charges confirmed. Do not consider a preferred date or time booked until you receive confirmation.

Can CivicBird guarantee a government certificate or approval?

No. CivicBird may assist with process guidance or task coordination, but eligibility, decisions and processing timelines remain with the relevant government department or authority.
