Pricing questions

Frequently asked questions

Important details about CivicBird's standard service fee.

Why is the standard total ₹354?

The standard service fee is ₹300. GST at 18% is ₹54, making the total ₹354 per task before any separately disclosed and agreed applicable charges.

Are government fees included?

No, not by default. Government or department fees, application charges, postage and similar official charges are separate unless explicitly included in a written quote.

Can the total cost be higher than ₹354?

Some tasks may involve travel or task-specific expenses. If any additional charge applies, it must be explained and agreed before the service is confirmed.

Does paying the service fee guarantee approval?

No. CivicBird provides assistance with the requested task; it cannot guarantee a government decision, eligibility, approval or processing time. Those are determined by the relevant authority.

Can I submit a request from this page?

Use the Request Assistance button to open the Services page and provide your task details. A request is not confirmed merely by reviewing the form; confirmation and payment features will be shown as the platform's booking system becomes available.

Need help with an office task?

Review the service options and submit your task details through CivicBird's Services page.
