Terms & conditions

Draft terms for review before public launch · Updated 16 October 2026

These starter terms need legal review and business-specific confirmation before launch.

1. Nature of service

CivicBird is intended to coordinate everyday, non-medical assistance between customers and screened local helpers (“Birds”). CivicBird is not a government department, ambulance, medical provider, law-enforcement agency or emergency service.

2. Requests and confirmation

Submitting a form or estimate does not create a confirmed booking. Availability, assignment, service area, timing and final charges must be confirmed by CivicBird.

3. Pricing, tax and travel

The advertised starting rate is ₹300 per hour plus 18% GST, subject to applicable tax treatment and final confirmation. Any travel or additional charges must be explained before the customer accepts the booking.

4. Customer responsibilities

Customers must provide accurate information, treat Birds respectfully, avoid unlawful requests and not share passwords, OTPs or banking credentials. Customers should confirm the assigned helper's identity before work begins.

5. Bird onboarding

Bird applications are subject to identity and background verification. The ₹149 application processing fee, refund conditions and payment flow must be displayed clearly in the live system before collection. Application does not guarantee approval or work.

6. Safety and emergencies

Do not use CivicBird for medical treatment, urgent emergency response or unlawful tasks. Contact official emergency services when needed. Report safety concerns to support.

7. Payments and cancellations

Online payments and cancellation/refund processes are not yet active on this demo site. A live version must publish clear cancellation, refund, grievance and payment terms before accepting money.

8. Contact and changes

CivicBird may update these terms when the service changes. Contact civicbird7@gmail.com or 9065760751 for questions.
