Safety at CivicBird

People, dignity and transparency come first.

🛡️

Identity and police verification

CivicBird's onboarding standard is that every active Bird must complete identity checks and police verification. Applications remain under review until the checks are completed. We will not describe a person as verified until the status is confirmed.

🪪

Check the assigned profile

Before starting, verify that the person matches the confirmed assignment. If anything differs, pause the task and contact CivicBird support.

🔐

Keep sensitive information safe

Never share OTPs, banking PINs, passwords or unnecessary documents with a Bird. Avoid handing over original identity documents unless an official process specifically requires it.

📞

Report a concern

For safety concerns, contact our support team at 9065760751 or civicbird7@gmail.com. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services/police directly.

Important: CivicBird is an assistance coordination service, not an ambulance, medical provider, law-enforcement service or emergency response service. For medical emergencies, call 112/108 as applicable.
