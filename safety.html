Our standards

Four foundations of a safer service experience

These are the standards CivicBird intends to follow as the service and its verification systems are implemented.

Bird identity and police verification

Every Bird is intended to complete identity review and required police verification before being activated for customer work. A profile or application alone is not proof that verification is complete.

Careful data handling

Personal information should be collected only for a defined purpose, access should be restricted, and sensitive documents should be handled through an appropriate secure process.

Transparent service terms

Customers should understand the requested task, standard fee, GST, any separate official fees and other agreed charges before the service is accepted.

A route to raise concerns

Customers should be able to report a concern about a request, representative, unexpected charge or interaction and receive instructions on the next step.

No promise of official approval

CivicBird cannot guarantee government decisions, eligibility or processing times. Those remain under the authority of the relevant department or institution.

Customer caution

Customers should verify the task and agreed charges, keep a record of important details and avoid sharing passwords, OTPs or banking credentials.

Protect yourself

Before you share information or agree to a task

A few practical precautions help protect your information and clarify expectations.

01

Never share passwords or OTPs

CivicBird should never need your banking password, card PIN or one-time password to assist with a civic task. Do not share them with a representative.

02

Handle identity documents carefully

Do not upload Aadhaar images or other sensitive documents into a general enquiry form. Submit documents only when required and through an approved secure workflow.

03

Confirm charges first

The planned standard service fee is ₹300 plus 18% GST (₹54), totalling ₹354. Government fees and other agreed costs may be separate and should be explained before proceeding.

04

Keep your request details

Once the online booking system is live, keep your official request reference, task details and bill for follow-up. Do not treat a draft or review screen as a confirmed booking.

Need to raise a safety concern?

Contact CivicBird with a clear description of the issue. Do not include passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive documents. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Questions

Safety FAQs

What customers should know about the policy and current website stage.

Will every Bird be police verified?

CivicBird's intended policy is that every Bird must complete required identity and police-verification checks before activation for customer work. Do not assume an individual is verified solely because they have applied.

Can I send Aadhaar details through the general service form?

No. Do not submit Aadhaar images or other highly sensitive identity documents through a general enquiry form. Use only a secure, approved document workflow when it is available and the documents are genuinely required.

Does CivicBird guarantee government approval?

No. Decisions, eligibility, processing times and approvals are controlled by the relevant authority. CivicBird can assist with a task but cannot guarantee the outcome.

Are online bookings and complaint tracking active now?

The full booking database, customer account tracking and in-site complaint workflow are planned for the backend integration phase. This information page does not submit or store a booking or complaint.

What should I do if I suspect fraud or feel unsafe?

Stop sharing information, do not pay an unexpected charge without clarification, and report the issue through CivicBird's official contact route. If there is immediate danger or suspected crime, contact the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement service.
