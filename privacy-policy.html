Privacy policy

Draft policy for review before public launch · Updated 16 October 2026

This is a starter policy, not legal advice. Have it reviewed for applicable Indian privacy and data-protection requirements before collecting personal data.

1. Information we may collect

A live CivicBird service may collect contact details, service requests, source and destination, account information, support messages, payment references and Bird onboarding documents when necessary.

2. Sensitive documents

Identity documents, including Aadhaar, are sensitive. This demo website does not upload or store submitted files. Before launch, CivicBird must implement secure transmission, restricted access, lawful purpose and consent, retention/deletion rules, and appropriate alternatives where available. Do not send Aadhaar through ordinary email or WhatsApp unless a legally reviewed secure process permits it.

3. Location

The demo asks for device location only after the user presses “Use my live location.” A live service should explain why location is needed and request permission before access.

4. Purpose and sharing

Information should be used only to manage accounts, fulfil assistance requests, verify Birds, process payments, provide support, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Share only what is necessary with assigned personnel and service providers.

5. Payments and security

Razorpay and Firebase are not connected in this build. A live implementation should use official integrations, avoid storing card/UPI credentials, and apply appropriate security safeguards.

6. Retention and your choices

A live service should retain data only as needed for stated purposes and legal obligations, and provide a way to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.
